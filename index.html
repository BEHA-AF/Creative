Lofio – Travel Booking App

The Problem

"When planning a trip, people waste a lot of time searching Google and YouTube just to figure out the right season to visit and how it fits their budget. Due to this delay, the season changes and the budget shoots up. Even if they sort that out, they still have to switch between multiple apps to find a good hotel or flight."

The Solution

"A smart platform that helps users understand the relationship between season and budget at a single glance. It helps users lock in a destination by clearly showing the best months to visit, and seamlessly lets them book budget-friendly hotels and flights all from one place."

My Role

Solo UI/UX Designer: "I entirely handled the ideation, season-budget flow design, visual layout, and Figma prototyping for this project. I also tested it within a small group to evaluate user feedback."

Lofio – Premium Real Estate Platform

The Problem

"The biggest challenge people face when trying to buy property through online platforms is the lack of security and fake listings. Since anyone can upload anything, the risk of scams increases significantly. Users struggle a lot to find a trustworthy agent or a secure property."

The Solution

"A design system made 100% secure by preventing just anyone from coming in and uploading a property. Every agent on the app follows strict guidelines and undergoes a thorough background check. The layout grants a Verified Badge only to those with high credibility and gives users complete freedom to check profiles and communicate before making a purchase."

My Role

Solo UI/UX Designer: "I completely handled the end-to-end ideation, immersive visual structure, verification flow design, and Figma wireframing for this project entirely on my own."

Upside – All-in-One Entertainment Platform

The Problem

"Users currently have to jump between multiple apps to watch movies, anime, and series, whether they want to see them in theaters or stream them on OTT platforms. Furthermore, moviegoers visiting theaters often waste time standing in long queues to buy food and drinks. It is also difficult to see a live total breakdown of the combined cost of tickets and food beforehand."

The Solution

"A unified design that brings theater booking and OTT streaming into a single app. Users choosing the OTT route can watch movies and series directly within the app via a 'Watch Now' button, without any external redirection. During theater booking, the layout enables users to pick their seats, date, and time, while seamlessly adding food and beverages to skip the queues. A dedicated side panel displays a live price update of the entire selection."

My Role

Solo UI/UX Designer: "I completely designed the end-to-end ideation, theater-OTT integration flow, live cart billing system, and visual UI design entirely on my own."

Choco Box – Premium Bakery & Custom Cake App

The Problem

"When buying cakes and desserts online, users are rarely able to fully customize them according to their imagination. Furthermore, existing apps lack simple options for users with food allergies or specific dietary restrictions (such as avoiding eggs) to clearly see if those ingredients are present or to easily exclude them from their order."

The Solution

"The 'Customized to Yourself' feature, inspired by social media avatar creation flows. Through this, users can completely customize the number of cake layers, flavors for each layer, fillings, toppings, and icing designs, while also setting their delivery date. Additionally, the design helps users entirely filter out ingredients they are allergic to or dislike, and clearly understand what goes into regular listings through detailed ingredient descriptions."

My Role

Solo UI/UX Designer: "I completely designed the end-to-end concept ideation, step-by-step cake customization flow, food allergy filtering logic, color palette design, and Figma prototyping entirely on my own."
